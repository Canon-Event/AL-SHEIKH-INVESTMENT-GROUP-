# Al Sheikh Investment Group — presentation notes

Use the 10-page PDF as a three-minute introduction, then show the working website. These are optional speaking notes, not additional claims about business performance.

1. **A stronger digital front door.** “Your group has a wide business scope. The website should communicate that breadth clearly, while retaining the identity people recognize. This is a working redesign using your existing identity and photography.”

2. **Why the website matters.** “After an introduction, prospects often want to understand who you are, find the right business and decide how to contact you. We designed the experience around those questions. The aim is to remove uncertainty and make that next conversation easier.”

3. **Before and after.** “The same Dubai photograph and gold seal remain. The new layout puts a clear introduction and business action into the first view. Navigation is more compact, and the page gives the visitor a direction.”

4. **Business discovery.** “There is one clear directory of seven businesses. Visitors can explore a relevant division and see its capabilities, focus and markets, then begin an enquiry with that business already selected.”

5. **Identity and trust.** “We preserved real company assets, cleaned the logo background and organized the image gallery. The company story and leadership are part of the experience. This gives visitors material to assess rather than relying on unsupported claims.”

6. **Mobile and languages.** “The five-language offering is retained: English, Arabic, French, Akan and Chinese. Arabic has a right-to-left layout. Visitors keep their page and context when changing language. Translations are drafts that need native approval before launch.”

7. **Enquiry journey.** “The enquiry journey carries the selected business into the form. This concept prepares an email draft that the visitor can open or copy. We would agree and connect the production delivery service as part of launch.”

8. **What is built.** “This is a working static site, with source and original assets ready for handover. We checked 65 content routes at five screen widths and ran accessibility samples. These are implementation checks; we have not claimed a measured sales or search-ranking increase.”

9. **Launch scope.** “The next work is clearly defined: company approval of facts and translations, the enquiry integration, official domain and indexing configuration, then agreed measurement and maintenance.”

10. **Close.** “The proposal is a clearer presentation of the group and an easier path to the right business. Let’s walk through the working concept and agree the final scope and launch plan.”

## Evidence and qualifications

The deck uses an archived original homepage capture and fresh screenshots of the current local build. Page counts and checks come from the delivered source and `al-sheikh/pitch/language-qa.json`. Ten automated accessibility samples reported zero Axe violations; that is not a full accessibility certification. Better clarity and reduced friction are design objectives. Validate commercial effects using agreed analytics and qualified-enquiry measures after launch. No ROI, ranking, speed-score or revenue uplift is promised.
