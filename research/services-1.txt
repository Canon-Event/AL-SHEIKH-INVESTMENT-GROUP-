Our Leading Petroleum Division
The Petroleum Department is one of our largest and most significant divisions, driven by our associate companies, KAS Petroleum and Al Sheikh Petroleum. KAS Petroleum specializes in providing premium petroleum products, including high-quality fuel and lubricants, while Al Sheikh Petroleum focuses on delivering exceptional petroleum services such as storage, distribution, and consultancy. Together, we ensure a seamless blend of quality, reliability, and client-focused solutions to meet the diverse needs of industries, businesses, and individuals, fueling progress and success every step of the way.
Delivering Excellence in Petroleum and Petrochemicals
KAS Petroleum is a leading provider of petroleum products and petrochemicals across the GCC, Middle East, and Africa. We specialize in the sale and purchase of LPG, LNG, crude oil, bitumen, sulfur, phosphate, gasoline, urea, fuel oil, and gas oil, catering to diverse industries and markets. Our LPG solutions serve clients in Pakistan and Bangladesh, while our sulfur products are in high demand in India, Egypt, and Turkey. With a strong network and commitment to quality, KAS Petroleum delivers reliable energy and raw material solutions to meet the needs of businesses worldwide.
Premier Maritime Services Provider
At Al Sheikh Petroleum, we pride ourselves on delivering exceptional, high-quality services that set us apart as a leader in the maritime industry. Our commitment to excellence, customer satisfaction, and a diverse range of offerings ensures we meet the unique needs of our global clients.
Our services include ship chartering, ship demolition, and ship sales and purchase, with a proven track record of selling numerous ships to breaker yards in India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. We also specialize in ship management,
including:
Cruise & Passenger Ship Management
LPG Ship Management
LNG Ship Management
Bulk Carrier Ship Management
Ship Crew Management

Lay-Up Ship Management

With our expertise and dedication, Al Sheikh Petroleum has firmly established itself as a trusted partner for comprehensive maritime solutions.

Comprehensive Petroleum, Maritime, and Cargo Solutions
At KAS Petroleum and Al Sheikh Petroleum, we offer an extensive range of services and products that cater to the diverse needs of our global clients. KAS Petroleum specializes in the sale and purchase of high-quality petroleum products, including LPG, LNG, crude oil, bitumen, sulfur, gasoline, urea, fuel oil, and gas oil, across the GCC, Middle East, Africa, and beyond. We also supply petrochemicals and cater to markets in Pakistan, Bangladesh, India, Egypt, and Turkey.
​
On the maritime front, Al Sheikh Petroleum stands as a leader in ship management and services, including ship chartering, demolition, sale and purchase, and dry docking at Dubai Dry Docks. We offer a wide range of ship management services such as cruise & passenger ship management, LPG & LNG ship management, bulk carrier management, crew and technical management, and lay-up ship management. Additionally, our cargo services ensure the safe and efficient transport of goods worldwide, handling a diverse range of materials across various regions.

Together, KAS Petroleum and Al Sheikh Petroleum are committed to providing reliable, high-quality solutions across the petroleum, maritime, and cargo industries, ensuring customer satisfaction and fostering long-term partnerships.
