The Story of KUSSAY AL SHEIKH
Mr. Kussay Al Sheikh is a distinguished Syrian businessman recognized for his visionary leadership and impact across multiple industries. With a career spanning over two decades, he has played a pivotal role in shaping the business landscape in the UAE and beyond.
Starting his journey in 1997, Kussay Al Sheikh built a strong foundation in business and investments before establishing Khuyool Group in 2005. Under his leadership, the group expanded into key sectors, including real estate, petroleum, diamonds, food, and medical equipment, solidifying its position as an industry leader.
Beyond his business success, Kussay Al Sheikh is dedicated to humanitarian efforts. He serves as a Goodwill Ambassador for IIMSAM under the United Nations, advocating for global initiatives against malnutrition. Additionally, he holds the esteemed title of Ambassador-at-Large, further extending his influence in international humanitarian work.
Today, Kussay Al Sheikh leads as the President and Chairman of Al Sheikh Investment Group, driving innovation, sustainability, and economic growth. His commitment to excellence and social impact continues to inspire and shape the future of multiple industries.
