## Platform

## Stack

Delegated: user selected our recommendation, a fast static website, and explicitly chose development directly in code. Build with semantic HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a dependency-free Node page generator. No required frontend runtime or third-party client scripts.

## Users

The requesting user needs a persuasive, reviewable client pitch to sell a website redesign. The intended company audience is prospective business partners, customers, and investors exploring the group's activities (inferred from its corporate website).

## Product Purpose

Replace the existing Wix presentation with a complete, more coherent corporate website. Explain the company's scope before inviting division-specific enquiries. Preserve real company content and assets.

## Operating Context

Al Sheikh Investment Group is based in Dubai, UAE. The source website names seven divisions: KAS Petroleum, KAS Properties, Diamonds & Gemstones, KAS Luxury Auction, Metals, Al Sheikh Petroleum, and Chemicals & Agricultural. Its logo states since 1997. Petroleum operations are described as active since 2003. Source website: https://www.alsheikhinvestmentgroup.com/ .

## Capabilities and Constraints

Complete group, about, division, gallery, and contact experiences. Responsive layouts and keyboard operation. Contact details must remain sourced. The pitch has no configured enquiry backend: prepare an email draft, explain that it is a draft, and never claim a message was sent. Publish a private pitch concept; do not replace the company's live domain. Do not fabricate projects, financial returns, people, partnerships, awards, testimonials, or metrics.

The user identified the original site's five languages: English, Arabic, French, Akan and Chinese. Preserve all five in the redesign. Arabic uses right-to-left layout. Page navigation and language switching must retain the selected page and enquiry/gallery context. Translated content is draft copy for the pitch and requires native review before company publication.

## Brand Commitments

Retain the name, real KAS/Al Sheikh gold seal logo, original company photographs and gallery assets. The user explicitly requested Impeccable's frontend design guidance and a complete visual redesign.
