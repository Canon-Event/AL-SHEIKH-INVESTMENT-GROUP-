## Editorial changes

Removed unfinished template text, repeated boilerplate, long duplicated descriptions and the notice recommending desktop use. Rewrote the navigation and content around the company’s actual businesses. Separated energy product trading from maritime services. Added a real leadership introduction using the original portrait. Avoided financial-return claims, unverified customer counts, fake partner logos, fabricated testimonials, live auction inventory, certification claims and unsupported humanitarian affiliations.

The original site also exposes English, Arabic, French, Akan and Chinese. The redesign now retains all five language choices, with a static version of every page in each language, Arabic right-to-left layouts and translated interactive labels. The translations are draft copy requiring native review before a company launch; English remains the editorial reference.
