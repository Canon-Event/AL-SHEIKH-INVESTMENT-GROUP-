# Client pitch guide

## Opening

“Your group operates across seven very different businesses. The website should make that breadth an advantage. I’ve created a new digital presentation that keeps your identity and photography while helping a prospective partner quickly understand the group, find the relevant division and start a useful conversation.”

## Three-minute walkthrough

1. **Start on the homepage.** The original Dubai photograph and gold seal establish the company immediately. Show the calm typography, clear navigation and strong business action.
2. **Move to the business explorer.** Focus or hover over property, gemstones and maritime. The large image changes with the selected business. Each row opens a complete division page.
3. **Open petroleum.** Show the clear distinction between the business, its capabilities, product focus and markets. Contrast this with the repeated descriptions in the existing petroleum page.
4. **Open the group profile.** The chairman’s original portrait and a concise company story provide a more personal introduction. No invented statistics are needed.
5. **Show the gallery on a phone.** Filter to gemstones or metals, open an image and move through the selection. The original gallery becomes useful evidence rather than a long carousel.
   Use the header language selector to show Arabic, French, Akan or Chinese on the same page; demonstrate the Arabic right-to-left layout. Present translated copy as a draft to be reviewed before launch.
6. **Start an enquiry from a division.** The relevant business is already selected. Explain that this presentation prepares an email draft; a production delivery service is part of the proposed launch scope.

## What the client is buying

| Current presentation problem | Redesign outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Seven businesses are hard to distinguish | One clear business directory and seven dedicated pages |
| Placeholder text and repeated descriptions weaken the presentation | Edited, structured company content |
| Gallery photographs are difficult to browse | Category filters and a keyboard-accessible image viewer |
| The notice recommends desktop browsing | Layouts checked from 320px to 1440px |
| Generic contact journey | Enquiry links carry the selected business into the form |
| Heavy source photographs and external font dependencies | Optimized, self-hosted assets and a dependency-free frontend |

## Suggested proposal scope

Offer a fixed project scope covering the design, content migration, responsive implementation, image optimization, enquiries integration, approved copy changes, domain deployment and handover. Define revision rounds and acceptance criteria in the proposal. Quote separately for ongoing maintenance, analytics, additional languages, CMS editing or future business pages. Agree exact deliverables and a delivery schedule with the client before promising them.

The most useful demonstration is the finished website. Present the mobile experience and business discovery flow before discussing technical implementation.

## Close

“This is already a working concept using your current assets and business information. The next step is to confirm the final company copy and enquiry process, then agree the launch and handover scope.”
